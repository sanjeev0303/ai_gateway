## Scope
Homepage `(marketing)/page.tsx` (Persuade, broadened after review: providers + gateway keys + CLI/SDK, not only security) and console overview `/app/overview` (Operate). Product renamed Sentinel -> Tollseal (user choice). Keep: the gateway prism, light and dark modes.

## Audience and job
Home: platform and security leads deciding whether to put a governed gateway in front of company AI. Action: Get started (sign up); secondary: see the workflow. Overview: health at a glance first (requests, spend, blocked, errors, latency), then what needs attention.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every AI provider behind one gateway key, and every request a document of value: inspected, then sealed and numbered. Not a security-only product: it is the hub where many providers connect and people and services call through one aigw_ key from the CLI, SDK or REST. The page refuses the category default (dark dev-tool hero, glow, feature-card grid) and the old warm editorial ivory world.
OWN-WORLD: Security printing. Cool security paper ground with fine guilloche linework, deep intaglio indigo ink, banknote green for the primary action, an iris foil (teal to blue to gold) reserved for the prism and the seal, intaglio red for blocked. Serial numbers and microprint in mono. Dark mode is night intaglio: indigo-black paper, luminous linework.
STORY: Visitors learn that every provider sits behind one gateway key, see how keys are issued and used from the CLI, SDK and REST, watch one request travel the checks (key, policy, guardrails, budget, route, record) and leave sealed, understand the gaps it closes (provider sprawl, keys everywhere, data leaks, surprise spend, no record), then act.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A banknote-shaped note spans the hero: headline set like a denomination at left, the prism in a guilloche rosette at right overlapping the note edge, serial number corners, a printed request ledger along the bottom band where each check stamps in; Get started is the seal-adjacent primary pill inside the note.
FORM: Assigned direction 5 of 7 (security printing); raises: ghost numerals for empty values (from seven-segment), streamed ledger and full keyboard diagrams (from ANSI BBS), fixed-tier problem statements (from pulp rack). Seed key 8c08fe6b.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
